Technology has always felt like a language worth mastering to me — one where ideas turn into working systems. As a Computer Science & Engineering student, I chase that feeling in every project, from machines that draw to systems that automate everyday friction.

But building things is only half the story. For over three years I've stood in front of classrooms, breaking down ICT and Bangla for students up to HSC level, because I believe knowledge multiplies when it's shared, not hoarded.

That belief carried me into leadership — running a science club, organizing a carnival, serving as a central prefect, and rising through the cadet ranks — every role teaching me that impact is a team sport. Today, I channel technology, creativity and leadership toward one goal: building things and people that last.
